## Research

My research follows people across three boundaries between services. Each is a line of work I have built over several years, through a series of studies rather than a single project. See [Research](research/) for more detail.

<div class="mindset-questions theme-cards">
  <section class="mindset-question">
    <span class="mindset-question-number">01</span>
    <h3>School and health</h3>
    <p>How schools and health services identify and support autistic and other neurodivergent children, and what follows for school attendance, exclusion, and hospital admission.</p>
    <p class="theme-evidence"><b>Key finding:</b> recorded autism incidence among more than 7 million pupils in England differed sharply between social and ethnic groups and between areas (<a href="https://doi.org/10.1016/S2352-4642(22)00247-4"><i>Lancet Child &amp; Adolescent Health</i></a>, 2022; joint first author). A 2026 OECD report cites it.<br><b>Projects:</b> HOPE-SEN, ADR UK fellowship (2024 to 2026).</p>
  </section>
  <section class="mindset-question">
    <span class="mindset-question-number">02</span>
    <h3>Mental health and addiction services</h3>
    <p>Who reaches treatment for drug and alcohol problems, particularly people who also have a mental illness, and how England's national treatment data can be used well.</p>
    <p class="theme-evidence"><b>Key findings:</b> national US studies of unmet need for mental health treatment and of access to opioid treatment (2019 and 2020; first author), and, in England, a study of mental health diagnoses and deprivation as predictors of access to opioid treatment (2023).<br><b>Projects:</b> UNITED, NIHR Team Science award (2026); anti-stigma course evaluation, NIHR (2026).</p>
  </section>
  <section class="mindset-question">
    <span class="mindset-question-number">03</span>
    <h3>Health and social care in severe mental illness</h3>
    <p>What NHS records show about treatment, harms, and inequalities for people with severe mental illness, and what linking health and social care records could add.</p>
    <p class="theme-evidence"><b>Key finding:</b> antipsychotic polypharmacy was associated with endocrine and metabolic adverse drug reactions among 13,391 patients (<a href="https://doi.org/10.1017/S0033291722000952"><i>Psychological Medicine</i></a>, 2023; joint first author).<br><b>Projects:</b> WISDOM, UKRI fellowship (2026 to 2029); patient observation on psychiatric wards, NIHR (2024 to 2028); health and social care data linkage, NIHR (from 2027).</p>

## The data behind the research

Linking records across services raises practical and ethical questions as well as statistical ones. Since 2019 I have helped run the [research database](https://www.northlondonmentalhealth.nhs.uk/research-database-) of what is now North London NHS Foundation Trust, first as Database Manager and now as Deputy Lead, including bringing two trusts' records together after their merger. I also sit on the Health Research Authority's Confidentiality Advisory Group, which advises on when confidential patient information can be used without consent. I can therefore advise collaborators on the whole course of a linked data study, from feasibility and approvals to analysis.
