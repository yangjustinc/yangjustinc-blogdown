My publications reflect the programme’s current empirical foundations in psychiatric epidemiology, linked administrative and clinical data, mental-health inequalities, neurodivergence, addiction, spatial analysis, and population health. These foundations support a wider multimodal agenda in which substantive questions determine when complementary evidence should be integrated across levels, settings, and timescales. The programme-defining outputs highlighted below are complemented by a dynamically generated view of recent work and my complete publication record from ORCID.

Programme-defining outputs

These papers illustrate the development of the main strands of my research programme and areas in which I have held substantial intellectual or analytical responsibility.

Clinical data and mental health services
Antipsychotic polypharmacy and adverse drug reactions among adults in a London mental health service, 2008–2018
Psychological Medicine · 2023

This study used routinely collected mental-health records to examine antipsychotic polypharmacy and adverse drug reactions in a large clinical population.

My contribution: First author; led the study examining prescribing and adverse outcomes using electronic health record data.

Administrative data and neurodivergence
Autism incidence and spatial analysis in more than 7 million pupils in English schools: a retrospective, longitudinal, school registry study
The Lancet Child and Adolescent Health · 2022

A national school-registry study of more than seven million pupils examining autism incidence and geographic variation across England.

My contribution: Joint first author; contributed population and spatial epidemiological analysis within a large interdisciplinary collaboration.

Addiction treatment and services
Responses among substance abuse treatment providers to the opioid epidemic in the USA: Variations in buprenorphine and methadone treatment by geography, operational, and payment characteristics, 2007-16
PLOS ONE · 2020

A national study examining how US substance-use treatment providers responded to the opioid epidemic, including geographic and organisational variation in access to opioid agonist treatment.

My contribution: First author; led the analysis of national treatment-provider data and interpretation of variation in evidence-based treatment provision.

Mental health inequalities
Demographic, socioeconomic, and health correlates of unmet need for mental health treatment in the United States, 2002–16: evidence from the national surveys on drug use and health
International Journal for Equity in Health · 2019

A population-based study examining demographic, socioeconomic, and health inequalities in unmet need for mental-health treatment in the United States over time.

My contribution: First author; led the epidemiological analysis of repeated national survey data on inequalities in access to mental-health treatment.

Addiction, inequality, and access to care
Investigating social deprivation and comorbid mental health diagnosis as predictors of treatment access among patients with an opioid use disorder using substance use services: a prospective cohort study
Substance Abuse: Treatment, Prevention, and Policy · 2023

A prospective cohort study examining social deprivation and comorbid mental-health diagnosis as predictors of treatment access among people with opioid use disorder.

My contribution: Contributed expertise in psychiatric epidemiology, addiction treatment, inequalities, and quantitative analysis.
