Since 2024 I have secured three nationally competitive awards as principal investigator or fellow, each built on questions I formulated. I also lead work packages on collaborative NIHR and HDR UK awards. Amounts are the funder’s contribution as published; my role is shown for each award, and the records are maintained through ORCID.

As principal investigator

Awards for which I am responsible for the scientific direction and delivery of the research.

Working collaboratively to Investigate the Socioemotional Drivers Of severe and enduring Mental illness
Principal Investigator; UKRI Mental Health Platform Cross-Hub Fellow
UKRI · £315,023 · Jan 2026 to Jan 2029

WISDOM brings together the Complex Emotions, DATAMIND, and Social Health hubs of the UKRI Mental Health Platform to study the social, emotional, environmental, and institutional drivers of severe and enduring mental illness.

Award UKRI3107
Using a National, Interdisciplinary Team to Enhance Drug and alcohol treatment data
Principal Investigator (named lead investigator); Work Package 3 Lead
National Institute for Health and Care Research · £98,937 · Jan 2026 to Dec 2026

UNITED brings together researchers, the national treatment providers Change Grow Live and Turning Point, and lived experience researchers to improve the research use of England’s National Drug Treatment Monitoring System. I led its scoping review of 83 research outputs and lead its work on principles for responsible use of the data.

Award NIHR306655
Health-related Outcomes, alternative Provision, and Exclusion among pupils with neurodivergent Special Educational Needs (HOPE-SEN)
Principal Investigator; ADR UK Fellow
Economic and Social Research Council · £148,211 · Sep 2024 to Sep 2026

HOPE-SEN used linked national education and hospital records (ECHILD) to study school absence, exclusion, alternative provision, and hospital admission among pupils receiving support for neurodevelopmental needs. Completed in September 2026; three first author papers are in preparation, and the analysis code is openly archived.

Award ES/Z503241/1
