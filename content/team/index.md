MINDSET (Multimodal Inference and Data Science for Epidemiology and Treatment) is my research group at UCL. It includes the postdoctoral research fellows whose research I direct day to day and the doctoral students I co-supervise. Much of our work depends on partnerships with NHS trusts, national data programmes, treatment providers, lived experience organisations, and other research groups, listed below.
