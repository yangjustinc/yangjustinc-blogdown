Biography

For event organisers and editors: this biography may be reused as written.

Dr Justin C Yang is a psychiatric epidemiologist and Senior Research Fellow in the Division of Psychiatry at University College London, and an Associate Editor of Addiction. Dr Yang’s research asks where inequalities in mental health arise: the points at which people’s needs are recognised or missed by services, and acted on or not, particularly when those needs span health and social care, mental health and addiction treatment, or health care and school. The work uses routinely collected records from these services, linked where possible, and addresses how such records can be used in ways the public can trust.

Dr Yang leads the MINDSET research group at UCL and an NIHR Team Science award on England’s national drug and alcohol treatment data (UNITED), holds a UKRI Mental Health Platform Cross-Hub Fellowship (WISDOM, 2026 to 2029), and led an ADR UK fellowship on the education and health of neurodivergent pupils (HOPE-SEN, 2024 to 2026). Dr Yang is Deputy Lead of the North London NHS Foundation Trust research database and an Expert Member of the Health Research Authority’s Confidentiality Advisory Group, and holds a PhD in Public Health and Primary Care from the University of Cambridge and an MSPH in Health Policy from Johns Hopkins University.
