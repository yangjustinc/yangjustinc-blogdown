Biography

For event organisers and editors: this biography may be reused as written.

Dr Justin C Yang is a psychiatric epidemiologist and Senior Research Fellow in the Division of Psychiatry at University College London, and Associate Editor of Addiction. Dr Yang’s research examines why the identification, treatment, and outcomes of mental health, neurodevelopmental, and substance use needs differ between groups and places, using England’s linked health, education, and treatment records.

Dr Yang is principal investigator of WISDOM, a UKRI Mental Health Platform Cross-Hub Fellowship; UNITED, an NIHR Team Science award on England’s national drug and alcohol treatment data; and HOPE-SEN, an ADR UK fellowship on the education and health of neurodivergent pupils. Dr Yang is Deputy Lead of the North London NHS Foundation Trust research database and an Expert Member of the Health Research Authority’s Confidentiality Advisory Group, and holds a PhD in Public Health and Primary Care from the University of Cambridge and an MSPH in Health Policy from Johns Hopkins University.
